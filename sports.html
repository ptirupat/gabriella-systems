Built for bat-and-ball sports

Gabriella's movement intelligence platform is designed for sports where bat, ball, and body all matter. We start with cricket and are building toward baseball and softball.

Where we play

Three sports. One platform.

Each sport shares a common structure — a bowler or pitcher, a batter, and a ball. Gabriella's computer vision stack is built around that shared structure, adapting metrics and key moments to each discipline.

Batting and bowling analysis from a single camera at the nets.

Working prototype

  • View-aware metrics — results follow the camera view you use
