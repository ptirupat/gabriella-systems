Sports

Built for bat-and-ball sports

Gabriella's movement intelligence platform is designed for sports where bat, ball, and body all matter. We start with cricket and are building toward baseball and softball.

Where we play

Three sports. One measurement platform.

Each sport shares a common structure — a bowler or pitcher, a batter, and a ball. Gabriella's computer vision stack is built around that shared structure, adapting metrics and phase detection to each discipline.

🏏

Cricket

Batting and bowling analysis from a single camera at the nets.

Working prototype
  • Batting: bat speed at impact, head stability, contact timing
  • Bowling: run-up speed, arm speed, front knee angle
  • Ball and bat tracking with SAM3 segmentation
  • COCO-17 pose estimation per frame
  • View-aware metrics — front-on and side-on
  • Quality gate: fail-loud, never invented numbers

One platform

The same pipeline. Sport-specific metrics.

All three sports share a common technical foundation — object detection, pose estimation, depth estimation, Kalman tracking, and a quality gate that only surfaces metrics when they are reliably measured. Sport-specific phase detection and metric definitions are layered on top.
