## Ludimos (ludimos.com)

**Class:** Phone-first AI cricket coaching + academy ops (consumer Skills/Showcase + Pavilion/360° AMS).

**Capture:** Phone only; Ball Tracking / Batting modes need stump calibration + tripod; Freeform AI for skeleton/drills. Docs require geometry; homepage often says “prop phone.” Auto action-clip from long sessions (dead time skipped).

**Capabilities (claimed/docs):** Bowling speed/line/length/deviation, pitchmap/beehive; batting footwork/shot type/connection, wagon wheel; stump line, trajectory, pose/kinogram; coach annotate (draw/voice/text); filters; longitudinal profiles; Skills challenges (legendary deliveries); leaderboards/referral credits; academy scheduling/comms/assessments.

**Pricing signals (uncertain — multiple stories in market):** Free ~300 clips/mo; Starter ~$149/yr (15 members); legacy academy tiers + app IAPs. Do not treat these as verified list prices.

**Strengths:** Distribution, freemium virality, AMS breadth, coach workflow maturity, elite logo marketing.

**Gaps vs Gabriella:** Phone CV + marketing “to the cm” without a public accuracy study; calibration friction; biomechanics = 2D overlay not lab-grade; weak fail-loud story; personal vs org continuity fog.

**Do not copy:** Absolute cm/broadcast claims without method; “no gear” when geometry matters; checklist maximalism; skeleton = deep biomechanics; fake testimonials.

**Gabriella wedge vs Ludimos:** Honest gated session metrics from nets video — not always-on phone-CV numbers. Session auto-crop is parity once shipped; do not claim it early.

## Kabuni (kabuni.com)

**Class:** Youth cricket development + AI coaching brand (India-first) — PlayOS + Super Coaches + schools + Kabuni Premier League. Different from Ludimos (pathway/content/league vs clip/AMS SaaS).

**Capture:** Press/site describe net-side motion + ball tracking device / PlayOS (not only phone-prop narrative). Real-time voice/visual/summary feedback; “1% improvement”; pro biomechanics comparison; gamification (K10). Public metric field list is thin — do not invent a catalog.

**GTM:** Founding schools, PE teachers, parents/juniors 12–16, Super Coach celebs (Ganguly, AB de Villiers, Watson, Iyer, etc.), national pathway/trials.

**Strengths:** Brand/celebrity access, school distribution, youth league pathway, real-time coaching UX story.

**Gaps vs Gabriella:** Thin public metric field list; proof story is access + cues, not gated coach session metrics; not positioned as academy CV lab first.

**Do not copy:** Super Coach content as substitute for measurable session truth; junior-league GTM as our wedge.

**Gabriella wedge vs Kabuni:** Coach-trusted gated session metrics vs access/cues/celebrity pathway. Treat Kabuni as pathway/brand, not as our metric competitor.
