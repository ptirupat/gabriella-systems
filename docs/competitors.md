## Ludimos (ludimos.com)

**Class:** Phone-first AI cricket coaching + academy ops (consumer Skills/Showcase + Pavilion/360° AMS).

**Capture:** Phone only; Ball Tracking / Batting modes need stump calibration + tripod; Freeform AI for skeleton/drills. Docs require geometry; homepage often says “prop phone.” Auto action-clip from long sessions (dead time skipped).

**Capabilities (claimed/docs):** Bowling speed/line/length/deviation, pitchmap/beehive; batting footwork/shot type/connection, wagon wheel; stump line, trajectory, pose/kinogram; coach annotate (draw/voice/text); filters; longitudinal profiles; Skills challenges (legendary deliveries); leaderboards/referral credits; academy scheduling/comms/assessments.

**Pricing signals (uncertain — multiple stories in market):** Free ~300 clips/mo; Starter ~$149/yr (15 members); legacy academy tiers + app IAPs. Do not treat these as verified list prices.

**Strengths:** Distribution, freemium virality, AMS breadth, coach workflow maturity, elite logo marketing.

**Gaps vs Gabriella:** Phone CV + marketing “to the cm” without a public accuracy study; calibration friction; biomechanics = 2D overlay not lab-grade; weak fail-loud story; personal vs org continuity fog.

**Do not copy:** Absolute cm/broadcast claims without method; “no gear” when geometry matters; checklist maximalism; skeleton = deep biomechanics; fake testimonials.

**Gabriella wedge vs Ludimos:** Honest gated session metrics from nets video — not always-on phone-CV numbers. Session auto-crop is parity once shipped; do not claim it early.

## Kabuni (kabuni.com)

**Class:** Youth cricket development + AI coaching brand (India-first) — PlayOS + Super Coaches + schools + Kabuni Premier League. Different from Ludimos (pathway/content/league vs clip/AMS SaaS).

**Capture:** Press/site describe net-side motion + ball tracking device / PlayOS (not only phone-prop narrative). Real-time voice/visual/summary feedback; “1% improvement”; pro biomechanics comparison; gamification (K10). Public metric field list is thin — do not invent a catalog.

**GTM:** Founding schools, PE teachers, parents/juniors 12–16, Super Coach celebs (Ganguly, AB de Villiers, Watson, Iyer, etc.), national pathway/trials.

**Strengths:** Brand/celebrity access, school distribution, youth league pathway, real-time coaching UX story.

**Gaps vs Gabriella:** Thin public metric field list; proof story is access + cues, not gated coach session metrics; not positioned as academy CV lab first.

**Do not copy:** Super Coach content as substitute for measurable session truth; junior-league GTM as our wedge.

**Gabriella wedge vs Kabuni:** Coach-trusted gated session metrics vs access/cues/celebrity pathway. Treat Kabuni as pathway/brand, not as our metric competitor.

## CricVision (cricvision.ai)

**Checked:** 2026-09-28 — public pricing and FAQ on [cricvision.ai](https://cricvision.ai); builder story in the [BOSC Tech Labs case study](https://bosctechlabs.com/case-study/data-led-cricket-coaching-system-with-ai-video-intelligence/).

**Class:** Academy OS + AI video coaching. Peer / academy competitor. Closer to Ludimos (academy platform + AI coaching) than to Gabriella’s fail-loud / view-aware nets session-metrics wedge.

**Capture:** Phone/smartphone-oriented. The public product is an academy app. The BOSC case study says coaches record on a smartphone or standard camera they already use, and claims no extra hardware, calibration, or setup. That “no calibration” line is a vendor claim — not a measured accuracy study.

**Capabilities (claimed/site):** White-label academy app and website; parent/coach chat; attendance and payments; one-tap PDFs and reels. CV on practice clips: stance, backlift, bat path, ball path, skeleton / body tracking, ball-by-ball segmentation, bat speed and ball speed, pitch maps. The case study also sells technique scores, coaching advice / drills, and academy ops dashboards.

**Built by:** BOSC Tech Labs (public case study).

**Pricing (public list on cricvision.ai, 2026-09-28):** Basic **$199/mo** — 3 coaches / 30 players. Pro **$399/mo** — 5 coaches / 75 players. These are the academy seat packs on the marketing site. No public accuracy figure is recorded here.

**Strengths:** Packaged academy ops (brand, parents, payments, attendance) plus visible AI coaching on practice clips. Clear public seat pricing for academy sales conversations.

**Gaps vs Gabriella:** Technique scores and coaching advice from phone/camera clips, without a public fail-loud / gated-metrics story. The product leads with white-label ops and AI feedback, not view-aware session metrics that stay blank when a take cannot be measured.

**Threat:** Medium GTM threat on academy sales. Low threat on the trust-metrics wedge unless they lead with calibrated fail-loud nets metrics.

**Do not copy:** White-label ops as our wedge; technique scores as gated session truth; “no calibration” as permission to publish untrusted numbers.

**Gabriella wedge vs CricVision:** Don’t race white-label ops. Own honest numbers from ordinary nets video.

## 3rd-Eye.TV (3rd-eye.tv)

**Checked:** 2026-09-28 — [3rd-eye.tv](https://3rd-eye.tv) / [www.3rd-eye.tv](https://www.3rd-eye.tv) (about, our-story, products, courses, company blog). Same public site on both hosts.

**Class:** Adjacent / low direct threat. Indian cricket technology company whose center of gravity is match-day officiating and broadcast DRS / association platforms, not phone or nets CV coaching metrics for academies. Peer class sits closer to Hawk-Eye / PitchVision match-analytics than to Ludimos or CricVision academy apps.

**Company (public):** About page: founded 2020; 3rd-Eye.TV and KGN Technologies are part of the sports division of HPMC Sales Limited. Founders named on the site: Navneet Saini and Valluri Kumar. Public story says V6 Sports Pvt. Ltd. is India’s first certified DRS provider. HQ/contact on the site: Plot 998, JLPL Industrial Area, Sector 82, Mohali, Punjab; info@hpmcsales.com.

**Capture:** Installed match DRS — high-frame-rate cameras, multi-cam replay, operators — not an upload-a-nets-clip product. Product listing: camera modes 4K @ 160 fps and HD @ 240 fps; DRS card says up to 8 cameras at 240 fps. Our-story stats (vendor): 8+ synchronized high-speed angles, 240 fps.

**Capabilities (claimed):** Core product is a certified Decision Review System. Company blog describes 3D ball tracking for LBW, ultra-slow-motion / high-frame-rate cameras, sound-based edge detection (SoundSense; Tribune advertorial: faint bat contact), front-foot no-ball detection, and waistline / full-toss monitoring. Site our-story lists ball tracking and trajectory, replay and third-umpire review, synchronized multi-angle recording, and umpire communication. Product card lists instant replay, multi-angle analysis, Soundsense, and LBW review metrics “Pitching in line,” “Impact,” and “Hitting in line,” and says replay is powered by Dartfish (described there as a FIFA- and UEFA-approved replay provider). Treat Dartfish / certification wording as vendor claims.

**Sports DeepMind (SDM), claimed:** Company blog describes an ecosystem beyond DRS: player and tournament management, live scoring, video analysis, and performance analytics, plus umpire/referee assessment and match/broadcast tools. LinkedIn company copy also lists match streaming and performance analytics for teams, athletes, and associations. This is not a public academy-nets SaaS page.

**Courses (public list prices, courses page, 2026-09-28):** Certified Cricket Video Analyst **₹9,999** (original price shown **₹14,999**). Certified Cricket Performance & Data Analyst **₹35,000**. These are analyst courses, not academy software seats.

**Pricing signals:** No public academy SaaS price comparable to CricVision’s $199 / $399 packs. Do not invent a platform or DRS day-rate.

**Scale (vendor claims on the company blog — not independently verified):** 1,800+ matches covered, 7,200+ hours streamed, 11K+ decisions reviewed, 2.2M+ deliveries processed. The homepage counter uses the same magnitudes and labels the 2.2M tile **Data Points**. Do not present either label as audited.

**Strengths:** Match-day DRS package (cameras, ball track, edge audio, third-umpire replay) aimed at boards, leagues, and umpires; association-platform language via SDM; analyst courses as a side motion.

**Gaps vs Gabriella:** Different buyer and job. Overlap is thin (ball track and “performance analytics” language). No public story of fail-loud, view-aware metrics from ordinary nets video.

**Threat:** Low direct threat to the nets upload / fail-loud session-metrics wedge. Watchlist if SDM coaching products expand into nets phone analysis.

**Do not copy:** Match-day DRS, broadcast review graphics, or association scale claims as if they were gated nets session metrics. Do not treat course fees as software pricing.

**Gabriella wedge vs 3rd-Eye:** They sell match officiating and association platforms. We sell honest numbers from ordinary nets video.
