Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gabriella Systems ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and analysis services at gabriellasystems.com.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, and organization name when you submit the contact or interest form.
  • Account data: Name, email address, and login activity for coach portal accounts.
  • Video uploads: Cricket or sports video clips uploaded through the coach portal.
  • Usage data: Standard web server logs including IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected via our hosting infrastructure.
  • Form submissions: Any information you voluntarily provide in the interest registration or contact forms.

2. How we use your information

  • To process video clips and return analysis outputs to you.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • To administer pilot programs with academies and coaches.
  • To improve our analysis software and services.

This website does not collect usage analytics and does not set cookies. If that changes, we will update this page.

3. Video uploads and analysis

When you upload a video clip through the coach portal, it is transmitted to our analysis infrastructure for processing. The resulting analysis output is returned in the portal.

Uploaded video is stored and processed on infrastructure hosted by Modal. Coach portal uploads are kept for the duration of the pilot plus 90 days, then deleted, or earlier on request. Analysis outputs are retained for the same period as their source video. We do not train on anyone's video without a written opt-in.

Only the academy's coaches with portal logins, plus Gabriella for support and processing, can see a player's clips.

4. Minor athletes and youth data

Our services may be used in academies that include athletes under 18 years of age. We take this seriously. See our Youth Athlete Data and Parental Consent policy for full details of how we handle video and data involving minors.

5. Data sharing and third-party processors

We use third-party cloud infrastructure to process video and host our services. These providers may have access to uploaded data as part of service delivery.

Our processors are Modal (hosting and video processing), GitHub Pages (website hosting), and Zoho (email). A data processing agreement is available on request at info@gabriellasystems.com.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

6. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold. However, no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to or stored on our systems.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at info@gabriellasystems.com. See also our Data Retention and Deletion policy.

8. Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not collect usage analytics. If that changes, we will update this page.

9. Contact

For privacy enquiries or data deletion requests, contact:
Email: info@gabriellasystems.com
Gabriella Systems, Florida, United States

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Continued use of our services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
