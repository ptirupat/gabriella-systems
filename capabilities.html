Product

Batting and bowling analysis from your nets video, filmed from more than one viewpoint.

Film from more than one angle, and Gabriella reads each view for the analysis it can see. Each section has a clip, a plain description, and when that result can be shown. The software works with the camera you have.

Batting

Ball tracking

Where did the ball travel?

A 3D path of the ball through the delivery, with no numbers on the path.

  • Front-on

Works when Front-on batting, and the ball is visible for enough of the delivery.

Pitch map

Where did it pitch?

An estimated map of where the ball bounced, shown as a rough length band. If the bounce is not clear, the card says it was not estimated.

  • Front-on

Works when Front-on batting, a bounce is seen, and the pitch edges are visible. Otherwise the card says it was not estimated.

Shot direction

Which way did the shot go?

An estimated direction for the shot — off, straight, or leg. Contact time is only a detail of that delivery, not a separate analysis. It is not drawn when contact is unclear or the ball is lost after contact.

  • Front-on

Works when Front-on batting, contact is clear, and the ball is still visible after contact.

Ball speed

A rough estimate, not a radar reading.

How accurate is it?

  • Front-on

Works when The ball is visible through enough of the flight.

Bat speed

An estimate from side-on video only.

An estimate of how fast the bat is moving at contact. Side-on is the view for this analysis.

  • Side-on

Works when Side-on batting, with the bat visible at contact.

Body pose

What was the body doing?

A pose overlay on the batter.

  • Front-on
  • Side-on

Works when The batter is in frame.

In development

  • Side-on footwork
  • Bat lift
  • Distance to the pitch of the ball
  • Head stability

Bowling

Front knee angle

An estimate of the bowler's front knee angle at landing.

  • Side-on

Works when A bowling delivery with the front leg visible at landing.

Run-up speed

An estimate of how fast the bowler is moving into the crease, from side-on video.

  • Side-on

Works when Side-on bowling, with the approach in view.

In development

  • Arm speed
  • Release

More batting and bowling analyses are being added as we train on more footage.

Accuracy

How accurate is it?

Gabriella estimates results from ordinary video. When we cannot trust a result, we say so and leave it blank. Ball speed is a rough estimate with a plus or minus 20 percent band, not a radar reading. Pitch map and shot direction are estimates from the ball path, not a survey of the pitch.
