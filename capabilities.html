Capabilities

What Gabriella estimates from your nets video

Six results from ordinary nets video. Each section has a clip, a plain description, batting or bowling tags, and the conditions that have to be true for a result to be shown.

Ball tracking

Where did the ball travel?

Gabriella draws the path of the ball through the delivery so coaches can see where it travelled.

  • Batting
  • Bowling

Works when The ball is visible for enough of the delivery.

Pitch map

Where did it pitch?

An estimated map of where the ball bounced, shown as a rough length band. If the bounce is not clear, the card says it was not estimated.

  • Batting

Works when Front-on batting, a bounce is seen, and the pitch edges are visible. Otherwise the card says it was not estimated.

Shot direction

Which way did the shot go?

An estimated direction for the shot — off, straight, or leg. It is not drawn when contact is unclear or the ball is lost after contact.

  • Batting

Works when Front-on batting, contact is clear, and the ball is still visible after contact.

Body pose

What was the body doing?

A pose overlay on the intended player, used for both batting and bowling.

  • Batting
  • Bowling

Works when The player is in frame.

Front knee angle

Was the front leg braced at landing?

An estimated angle of the bowler's front knee at landing, labelled as an estimate.

  • Bowling

Works when A bowling delivery with the front leg visible at landing. Shown as an estimate.

Ball speed

How fast was it?

A rough estimate of ball speed with a plus or minus 20 percent band. It is not a radar reading.

  • Batting
  • Bowling

Works when The ball is visible through enough of the flight. This is a rough estimate with a ±20% band, not a radar reading.

Accuracy

How accurate is it?

Gabriella estimates results from ordinary video. When we cannot trust a result, we say so and leave it blank. Ball speed is a rough estimate with a plus or minus 20 percent band, not a radar reading. Pitch map and shot direction are estimates from the ball path, not a survey of the pitch.
