Capabilities

What Gabriella measures from your nets video

Six results are available now. Each section has a clip, a plain description, batting or bowling tags, and the conditions that have to be true for a result to be shown.

Ball tracking

Available now

Where did the ball travel?

Gabriella draws the path of the ball through the delivery so coaches can see where it travelled.

  • Batting
  • Bowling

Works when The ball is visible for enough of the delivery.

Pitch map

Available now

Where did it pitch?

An estimated map of where the ball bounced, shown as a rough length band. If the bounce is not clear, the card says it was not measured.

  • Batting

Works when Front-on batting, a bounce is seen, and the pitch edges are visible. Otherwise the card says it was not measured.

Shot direction

Available now

Which way did the shot go?

An estimated direction for the shot — off, straight, or leg. It is not drawn when contact is unclear or the ball is lost after contact.

  • Batting

Works when Front-on batting, contact is clear, and the ball is still visible after contact.

Body pose

Available now

What was the body doing?

A pose overlay on the intended player, used for both batting and bowling.

  • Batting
  • Bowling

Works when The player is in frame. This is the unifier across batting and bowling.

Front knee angle

Available now

Was the front leg braced at landing?

An estimated angle of the bowler's front knee at landing, labelled as an estimate.

  • Bowling

Works when A bowling delivery with the front leg visible at landing. Shown as an estimate.

Ball speed

Available now

How fast was it?

A rough estimate of ball speed with a plus or minus 20 percent band. It is not a radar reading, and we do not headline a tighter number.

  • Batting
  • Bowling

Works when The ball is visible through enough of the flight. This is a rough estimate with a ±20% band, not a radar reading.

Accuracy

How accurate is it?

Gabriella estimates results from ordinary video. When we cannot trust a result, we say so and leave it blank. Ball speed is a rough estimate with a plus or minus 20 percent band, not a radar reading. Pitch map and shot direction are estimates from the ball path, not a surveyed measurement.

Ball

  • Length label. A plain length word — for example full, good, or short.
  • Bounce line (off / middle / leg). Whether the ball pitched off, middle, or leg.
  • Session pitch-map heatmap and wagon wheel. Grouping and scoring areas across a whole session.
  • Deviation off the pitch. Movement from the angle change at the bounce. We will not claim swing or spin until that is actually computed.
  • Release-to-contact time. How long the batter had to react, when both release and contact are in frame.

Batter

  • Head stability. Head movement from release to contact.
  • Head over base. Head offset from the front foot or feet midpoint at contact.
  • Front foot or back foot. Direction the front ankle moves from stance to contact.
  • Stride length and stance width. Ankle separation as a fraction of leg length. We will not invent height.
  • Front knee bend at contact. The batter’s front knee angle at contact.
  • Backlift height. Peak wrist height versus the shoulder.
  • Contact point relative to the body. Contact versus the head and front foot. Copy will not say early or late as timing.
  • Bat speed. Speed of the bat at impact. This is not an Available now card.

Bowler

  • Knee brace through release. Front knee angle from landing to release: braced, flexing, or extending.
  • Delivery stride length. Back-foot to front-foot landing as a fraction of height. We will not invent height.
  • Trunk side bend at release. Shoulder-to-hip line versus vertical.
  • Trunk forward lean at release. The same line from the side.
  • Arm slot at release. Shoulder-to-wrist line versus vertical.
  • Relative release height. Wrist height at release as a percent of standing height, not an absolute metre value.
  • Front arm height. Non-bowling wrist height at back-foot landing.
  • Run-up rhythm. Step count and step-timing consistency from the ankles.
