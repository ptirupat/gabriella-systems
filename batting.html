Coaching questions, not engineering metrics

Answers the questions coaches actually ask

Where did this ball pitch?

Pitch map is an estimated picture of where the ball bounced. The length bands are a rough guide and are not checked for this net.

Which way did the shot go?

Shot direction is an estimated line from the nets. Shots missed or lost after contact are not drawn.

How fast was the bat?

Bat speed is a pipeline estimate for this take. It is labelled as an estimate, not a measured grade.
