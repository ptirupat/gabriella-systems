Answers the questions coaches actually ask

When did contact happen in this take?

Contact timing in this take — measured in milliseconds from clip start to bat-ball contact. Useful as a same-view marker across sessions, not an early/late judgment.

Is my head staying still through the shot?

Pose overlays help coaches review head and upper-body stability through the shot — alongside bat and ball tracking where visible.

How consistent is my stride across the session?

Compare stride length, foot position, and weight transfer across 20, 50, or 100 deliveries in a single session.
