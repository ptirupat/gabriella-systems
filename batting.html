Coaching questions, not engineering metrics

Answers the questions coaches actually ask

How fast was the bat at contact?

How fast the bat was moving at contact in this take (km/h).

Is my head staying still through the shot?

How much the head moved from downswing to contact (cm). Lower usually means steadier — not a technique grade.

When did contact happen in this take?

Time from the start of this take to contact (ms). Use it to compare same-view sessions — not early vs late.
