Cricket Vision Demo

Live batting analysis

Pick a sample clip or upload your own machine-end batting video. The system tracks the ball and bat, estimates 17-point player pose, and reports ball & bat speed, depth, and the impact frame — all from a single camera.

Sample clips play instantly. Uploads are processed live on GPU (first run warms up the models, ~1–3 min). Speeds are camera-only estimates.

Your video stays private. Uploaded clips are used only to generate your analysis.
