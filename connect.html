How you can register interest

Submitting interest through any pathway does not create an employment, contractor, advisory, partnership or compensation relationship.

Pathway 1

Pilot Interest

Coaches · Academies · Clubs · Athlete development programs

Interested in helping evaluate how video intelligence could support real coaching environments? Organizations may register interest in future pilot discussions involving cricket, baseball or softball.

Pathway 2

Research Conversations

Universities · Laboratories · Established researchers

We welcome preliminary conversations about institutionally approved research related to athlete movement, sports video understanding, responsible AI and performance evaluation. Any collaboration would require separate institutional, legal, data-rights and authorship agreements.

Pathway 3

Advisory Interest

Coaching · Biomechanics · Responsible AI · Sports technology · Commercialization

Subject-matter experts may introduce their background for consideration if a formally scoped advisory need arises. Submitting interest does not establish an advisory appointment or obligation for either party.

Pathway 4

Future Paid Opportunities

Engineers · Computer-vision researchers · Designers · Commercial professionals

Gabriella Systems is not currently hiring. Professionals may register to receive notice if relevant paid opportunities become available in the future. Participation does not constitute an application or employment relationship.
