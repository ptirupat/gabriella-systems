Youth Athlete Data & Parental Consent

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Gabriella Systems' services may be used by academies, clubs, and coaches working with athletes under 18 years of age. This policy explains how we handle video and data involving minors, and what obligations apply to parties using our services with young athletes.

Key requirement

Before uploading video of any athlete under 18 to Gabriella Systems' services, the uploading party must obtain verifiable written consent from a parent or legal guardian of that athlete. Gabriella Systems relies on the academy, coach, or organization to obtain and retain this consent.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to any person or organization that uploads, submits, or shares video content featuring athletes under 18 years of age through the coach portal or any other Gabriella Systems service, including a pilot program.

2. Parental consent requirements

Academies and coaches using Gabriella Systems' services with athletes under 18 must:

  • Obtain written consent from a parent or legal guardian before submitting video of that athlete;
  • Inform the parent or guardian that the video will be processed by computer vision software;
  • Retain a copy of the consent record;
  • Make that record available to Gabriella Systems on request;
  • Honour any withdrawal of consent — including requesting deletion of the athlete's data from Gabriella Systems.

Academies obtain parental consent. A template can be shared on request at info@gabriellasystems.com.

3. What information is collected about young athletes

The Services process video frames to generate movement analysis outputs. We do not require or request the name, age, school, or other personal identifiers of individual athletes. However:

  • Video may contain identifiable biometric imagery (face, body) of young athletes;
  • Analysis outputs may be associated with a player identifier provided by the academy;
  • We do not create individual profiles of named athletes without explicit consent from the academy.

4. How we handle youth athlete video

Youth athlete video is retained for the shortest period that applies to how it was submitted, as set out in our Data Retention and Deletion policy. Video uploaded through the coach portal is stored on Modal and kept for the duration of the pilot plus 90 days, then deleted, or earlier on request. Youth athlete video is never used for training or marketing without written parental and academy consent. Only the academy's coaches with portal logins, plus Gabriella for support and processing, can see a player's clips.

Academies may request deletion of a young athlete's video data at any time by contacting info@gabriellasystems.com. See our Data Retention and Deletion policy for timelines.

5. Age-appropriate use of analysis outputs

Performance analysis outputs about young athletes should be used in age-appropriate ways. We recommend:

  • Analysis is reviewed by a qualified coach before being shared with young athletes;
  • Quantitative metrics are presented in a developmental context, not as rankings or judgments;
  • Young athletes are not shown outputs that may cause unnecessary anxiety or pressure;
  • Analysis is treated as a coaching support tool, not a selection or progression criterion.

6. Responsibility

Gabriella Systems provides a software analysis tool. The academy, club, or coach using the tool is responsible for ensuring proper consent is in place and that analysis outputs are used appropriately and responsibly with young athletes.

7. Contact

Questions about youth data handling or consent: info@gabriellasystems.com
