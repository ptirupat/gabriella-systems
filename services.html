Cricket Vision Platform

A CV module for bowling-machine batting sessions

A focused computer vision system for academies, clubs, and players who want objective batsman feedback from the machine end of the net.

Vision Module

Mount a camera module above or below a bowling machine to capture a repeatable view of the batsman, ball path, bat, and wicket zone.

  • Consistent batsman-facing camera angle
  • Works with machine-delivered practice balls
  • Designed for net sessions and repeatable drills

Tracking Pipeline

Every clip is turned into coach-ready detail: where the ball and bat moved, how the batsman's body responded, and how fast each delivery and shot traveled.

  • Ball and bat path, trail, and contact point
  • Full-body pose landmarks for batsman mechanics
  • Ball and bat speed estimates from a single camera

How it works technically: ball & bat segmentation, YOLO-pose landmarks, depth estimation, and multi-model aggregation.

Batsman Feedback

Turn the analyzed clip into practical feedback for the batsman: what moved, when impact happened, and which correction should come next.

  • Impact frame and contact-zone review
  • Head, shoulder, wrist, and foot movement cues
  • Session trends across repeated deliveries
