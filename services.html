Why a dedicated capture device is planned

Consistent measurement benefits from consistent capture. Today Gabriella analyzes cricket clips in software; purpose-built hardware is planned to improve repeatable net capture beyond a typical phone setup.

  • Batting and bowling clip analysis
  • Pose, bat, and ball overlays where visible
  • View-aware metrics for the camera view
  • Phase breakdown for the action in frame
  • Analysis of a submitted clip
  • Portable capture hardware on the roadmap
