Position Gabriella Vision for what you want to measure

Batting Mode — Device at the bowling end facing the batter
Captures: Ball trajectory + Bat path + Batter pose
Measures: Ball speed, bat speed, impact timing, body mechanics
Bowling Mode — Device repositioned side-on facing the bowler
Captures: Run-up + Bowling action + Release + Ball
Measures: Run-up speed, arm speed, knee angle, ball speed

Built for cricket, not adapted to it

Why a dedicated device exists

Reliable performance measurement requires reliable capture. Purpose-built hardware enables Gabriella to control what a phone camera cannot.

  • Optimised camera positioning for cricket
  • High frame rate for fast-moving objects
  • Calibrated field of view
  • Consistent session-to-session capture
  • Multi-camera capability
  • Future sensor integration
  • Automated operation — no camera operator needed
