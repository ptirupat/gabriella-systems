About Gabriella Systems

Computer vision infrastructure for cricket performance

We build cricket-specific vision systems that measure batting and bowling performance with the consistency required for genuine progress tracking.

Our Focus

Gabriella Systems focuses on cricket because the sport rewards tiny technical changes: a front shoulder opening too soon, a front knee collapsing at contact, a head falling across the line, or release timing shifting by frames.

Our platform turns practice sessions into measurable patterns — giving coaches a consistent view of what changed between deliveries, drills, and sessions, for both batters and bowlers.

Better cricket feedback starts when video stops being just footage and becomes structured evidence.

What guides us

Designed around the realities of cricket practice

Accessible Capture

Useful analysis should work from a portable vision system placed at the nets — without changing how players train.

Measurable Progress

Players need trends and repeatable benchmarks, not one-off impressions after a single good or bad delivery.

Coach-Led Insight

The technology supports coaching judgment by surfacing motion evidence, priorities, and comparisons faster.
