About Gabriella Systems

Computer vision infrastructure for cricket performance

Gabriella Systems is cricket-first computer vision for batting and bowling. We measure what the take supports — and say so when it does not.

What is Gabriella Systems?

Gabriella Systems builds computer vision for cricket nets. Coaches upload or review batting and bowling clips; the platform detects the player, bat, and ball where they are visible, then returns overlays and metrics suited to that action and camera view.

Numbers are fail-loud. If detection, tracking, or pose is below the quality gate, the HUD shows Can't measure rather than a guessed value. We started with cricket. Baseball and softball are the expansion we want next — same bat-and-ball problems, not a claim that those sports ship today.

Better cricket feedback starts when video stops being just footage and becomes structured evidence.

Mission, vision, leadership

What we are building, and why

Mission

Give cricket coaches objective, comparable evidence from practice — pose, bat, and ball when the take supports it, and an honest Can't measure when it does not.

Vision

A portable vision system that makes session-to-session cricket improvement measurable, then carries that same trust into baseball and softball.

Leadership

Founded and led by Praveen Tirupattur. Gabriella is a founder-led product company: cricket performance vision first, then the same bat-and-ball stack toward baseball and softball.

Our Focus

Gabriella Systems focuses on cricket because the sport rewards tiny technical changes: a front shoulder opening too soon, a front knee collapsing at contact, a head falling across the line, or release timing shifting by frames.

Our platform turns practice sessions into measurable patterns — giving coaches a consistent view of what changed between deliveries, drills, and sessions, for both batters and bowlers.

We would rather leave a metric blank than publish a number the take cannot support.

What guides us

FAQ

Gabriella Systems, in brief

What is Gabriella Systems?

A cricket-first computer vision company. We turn batting and bowling clips into overlays and metrics coaches can compare — and we fail loud when a take is not good enough to measure.

What sports does Gabriella support today?

Cricket is live: batting and bowling clip analysis in the Showcase. Baseball and softball are planned expansion, not current products.

What does fail-loud mean?

If detection, tracking, or pose misses the quality gate, we show Can't measure or omit the field. We do not invent a speed, angle, or time.
