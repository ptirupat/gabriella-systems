About Gabriella Systems

Every athlete deserves structured evidence, not just impressions

Gabriella Systems builds computer vision infrastructure for bat-and-ball sports — making movement intelligence accessible, measurable, and trustworthy.

Who we are

Gabriella Systems builds computer vision infrastructure for cricket — and, in time, for baseball and softball. We combine computer vision and trustworthy AI so that coaches and athletes have objective performance evidence from ordinary practice video.

Mission

Make movement intelligence accessible and trustworthy for every cricket team, academy, and athlete

We build AI systems that turn ordinary practice video into structured, honest performance evidence — so coaches can measure what actually changed, and athletes can understand how.

Vision

A future where every athlete is measured accurately, fairly, and with full transparency

AI in sport earns trust by being explicit about what it can measure, what it cannot, and why — across every camera view, every skill level, and every stage of athletic development.

Why we exist

The problem we set out to solve

Most cricket coaching still relies on video as a memory aid, not as structured data.

Technical changes in batting and bowling are often small — a shoulder opening a fraction earlier, a knee angle shifting by a few degrees, a release timing changing by a handful of frames. These details are difficult to see consistently in real time and difficult to recall reliably from session to session.

Place the camera where the coach needs it, so Bat and Head come from a view that can see them, and get evidence that matches that view. Results follow the camera view you use. We do not invent a number the camera cannot see.

Accessible capture

Analysis should work from ordinary video at the nets — no specialist hardware required to start.

Honest metrics

Metrics follow the camera view. We do not invent a number when that view cannot support it.

Progress, not snapshots

Single-session impressions are not useful. Reproducible trends across sessions are.

Coaches stay in control

Our system surfaces evidence and supports coaching judgment — it does not replace it.

What we stand for

Our values

Place the camera for the view

Place the camera where the coach needs it, so Bat and Head come from a view that can see them. An estimated pitch map, estimated shot direction, and ball track are available when the camera view supports them. Pitch map and shot direction are coming soon in the production app; examples are labelled estimated.

Evidence, not impressions

Coaches deserve structured data they can compare across sessions, not one-off visual impressions that are hard to recall or repeat.

Clear about each view

We say what a camera view can show. When the view cannot support a metric, we do not invent a number.

Human authority matters

AI should support coaches, not replace them. Our system surfaces evidence and priorities — the coaching decision remains with the coach.

Progress over ranking

The most useful comparison is an athlete against their own baseline, not against others. Individual progress is the goal, not league tables.

Research-grounded practice

Our measurement system is built on active research in computer vision, movement representation, and trustworthy AI — not assumptions.

Research

Product and research, together

Gabriella's product is built on active research — in visual AI, movement representation, uncertainty-aware measurement, and responsible AI for sport. Every improvement to how we find, track, and measure feeds back into the system athletes and coaches use.

We also study harder questions — responsible AI for sport, trustworthy measurement, and what good AI design looks like when the athlete is still developing.

Where we are going

Building toward a full movement intelligence platform

Today Gabriella analyzes cricket batting and bowling from a single camera. Each phase of our roadmap extends both the depth of analysis and the consistency of capture — until every training session becomes a structured record that grows over time.

Live today

Batting & bowling video analysis

Pose, bat, and ball tracking with view-aware metrics and overlays from cricket clips in the Showcase. The camera view decides what we can show; we do not invent a number when it cannot.

Next

Cleaner delivery-by-delivery review flows, session-level analysis from long match video, and structured reporting for coaching staff.

Hardware

Future

The movement intelligence platform is designed to extend to baseball and softball. Research is already underway to support this transition.
