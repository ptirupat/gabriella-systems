About Gabriella Systems

Cricket session metrics for coaches and academies

Gabriella measures batting and bowling from practice clips so coaches at the nets can review what actually changed — not just watch footage again.

What is Gabriella?

What Gabriella measures now

Gabriella Systems is cricket vision for nets. Coaches and academies upload batting or bowling clips and get pose, bat, and ball overlays plus session metrics that fit the camera view. Ungated takes fail loud — Can't measure — rather than a guessed number.

We started with cricket; baseball and softball are next (same session-metric / fail-loud approach). Roadmap — we do not support baseball or softball today.

Better cricket feedback starts when video stops being just footage and becomes structured evidence.

What we stand for

Our values

Fail loud, not silently

When our system cannot reliably measure something, it says so — clearly and without substituting a guess. "Can't measure" is a valid and honest result.

Evidence, not impressions

Coaches deserve structured data they can compare across sessions, not one-off visual impressions that are hard to recall or repeat.

Transparency about limitations

Not every metric is measurable from every camera view. We are explicit about what we can and cannot determine, and why.

Human authority matters

AI should support coaches, not replace them. Our system surfaces evidence and priorities — the coaching decision remains with the coach.

Progress over ranking

The most useful comparison is an athlete against their own baseline, not against others. Individual progress is the goal, not league tables.

Research-grounded practice

Our measurement system is built on active research in computer vision, movement representation, and trustworthy AI — not assumptions.
