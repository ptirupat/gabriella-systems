About Gabriella Systems

Computer vision for cricket performance

We started with cricket. Gabriella measures batting and bowling from practice clips so coaches can review what actually changed — not just watch footage again.

What is Gabriella?

A cricket-first performance vision company

Gabriella Systems is a cricket-first computer vision company. Coaches upload batting or bowling clips and receive pose, bat, and ball overlays plus view-aware metrics from the Showcase pipeline. Portable capture hardware, baseball, and softball analysis are on the roadmap — they are not shipping products today.

The software is live for cricket batting and bowling video analysis. Session comparison is coach-led and strongest when takes share a similar view and quality.

Better cricket feedback starts when video stops being just footage and becomes structured evidence.

Mission, vision, and lead

What we are building, and who leads it

Mission

Give cricket coaches objective, view-aware evidence from batting and bowling clips — so practice becomes measurable, not just footage.

Vision

Become the performance-vision layer for bat-and-ball sports. Cricket first; baseball and softball as the same measurement ideas travel. Those sports are roadmap, not a shipping claim.

Leadership

Gabriella is a small, founder-led team. We build cricket-first computer vision and work directly with academies on pilots. For press or partnership, write admin@gabriellasystems.com.

What guides us

Designed around the realities of cricket practice

Accessible Capture

Useful analysis should work from a portable vision system placed at the nets — without changing how players train.

Measurable Progress

Players need trends and repeatable benchmarks, not one-off impressions after a single good or bad delivery.

Coach-Led Insight

The technology supports coaching judgment by surfacing motion evidence, priorities, and comparisons faster.
