Data Retention & Deletion

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how long Gabriella Systems retains different types of data and how you can request deletion.

1. Data we hold

Data type Retention period Notes
Contact form submissions Up to 24 months from submission Used to respond to inquiries; deleted on request
Video uploads (coach portal / pilot program) Duration of the pilot plus 90 days Uploaded through the coach portal and stored on Modal. Then deleted, or earlier on request.
Analysis outputs Same period as the source video Retained for the same period as the originating upload.
Server/access logs Up to 90 days Standard infrastructure logging

Video uploaded through the coach portal is stored on Modal and kept for the duration of the pilot plus 90 days, then deleted, or earlier on request. Analysis outputs follow the same period as their source video.

2. Deletion requests

To request deletion of any data we hold about you or your athletes, contact us at:

Email: info@gabriellasystems.com
Subject line: Data Deletion Request

We will confirm receipt within 5 business days and action the deletion within 30 days unless a legal retention obligation applies.

For requests relating to athletes under 18, please also see our Youth Athlete Data and Parental Consent policy.

3. Backups and residual copies

We keep no separate backups of uploaded video.

4. Contact

Data retention and deletion inquiries: info@gabriellasystems.com
