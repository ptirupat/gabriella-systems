2. Deletion requests

To request deletion of any data we hold about you or your athletes, contact us at:

Email: info@gabriellasystems.com
Subject line: Data Deletion Request

We will confirm receipt within 5 business days and action the deletion within 30 days unless a legal retention obligation applies.

For requests relating to athletes under 18, please also see our Youth Athlete Data and Parental Consent policy.

3. Backups and residual copies

After deletion, residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for a period before being overwritten as part of standard backup rotation. We will not access or use residual backup copies of deleted data for any purpose.
