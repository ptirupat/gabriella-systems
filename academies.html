Academy pilot programme

What the pilot involves

We are selecting academies for a structured pilot of Gabriella Vision. Here is exactly what participation involves.

What the academy receives

  • Access to the Gabriella Vision analysis software for the pilot period
  • Batting and bowling analysis reports for agreed sessions
  • Per-clip overlays: pose, bat, and ball tracking where detectable
  • View-aware metrics with qualification notes where measurement is conditional

Supported recording setup

  • Existing smartphone, tablet, or fixed camera at the nets
  • Side-on or front-on view (affects which metrics are available)
  • Minimum 30 fps recommended for reliable tracking
  • Good lighting and unobstructed view of player, bat, and ball

Pilot duration and scope

Duration and number of sessions are discussed individually. A typical pilot covers a defined number of net sessions over several weeks with a small cohort of athletes.

What Gabriella measures

  • Batting: bat speed at impact, contact time, head stability, ball speed (quality-gated)
  • Bowling: run-up speed, arm speed, front-knee angle at plant
  • Pose overlays across the action
  • Phase breakdowns where the take supports it

What is still experimental

  • Session-to-session comparison (requires consistent camera placement)
  • Bounce point and contact location (view-dependent)
  • Large-session trend analysis across many deliveries
  • Purpose-built hardware integration (on the roadmap)

Data ownership and privacy

Video uploads and analysis outputs belong to the academy. Gabriella Systems does not use pilot session videos for model training without separate written consent. Data handling policies are being finalised — contact info@gabriellasystems.com with questions.

Pilot success criteria

Defined together at the start of the pilot — typically: analysis pipeline runs on all submitted clips; at least the primary metrics are produced for each session; coach feedback captured. No improvement guarantees are made.

Pricing: Pilot terms discussed individually. No pricing has been published. Submitting a pilot request does not create any commercial obligation for either party.
