For Academies

One system. Every player. Objective progress.

We’re piloting Gabriella with cricket academies. The pilot uses existing cameras and Gabriella software to bring batting and bowling analysis to your nets — without specialist hardware to start.

Who it’s for

Coaches, players, and the academy

Coaches

Review batting and bowling with the same results on every player. Compare sessions when the camera position is the same, and keep a clear record of what was measured — and what was not.

Players

See structured evidence from ordinary nets video, not just a memory of the session.

Academy

Build a consistent record across the squad. Session-to-session comparison is still in development until real, same-position data exists.

The pilot

What the pilot involves

We are selecting academies for a structured pilot. Purpose-built capture hardware is not required to take part.

  • Access to Gabriella software for the pilot period
  • Batting and bowling analysis from ordinary camera recordings
  • The six Available now results, with a clear note when something cannot be measured
  • A conversation about how the results fit your coaching workflow
