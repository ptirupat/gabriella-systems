One delivery is useful. A season is powerful.

Planned player histories and session comparisons aim to help coaches explore changes over time. These examples illustrate the roadmap, not current results.

Batting — Session Progress

Head displacement 6.4 cm → 4.1 cm
Front stride 51 cm → 58 cm
Bat speed 87 → 92 km/h
Impact timing ±120 ms → ±60 ms

Bowling — Session Progress

Ball speed 119 → 127 km/h
Front-knee angle 31° → 16°
Run-up speed 19.2 → 21.5 km/h
Stride length 1.68 → 1.82 m

Illustrative roadmap data, not measured customer results. Changes do not establish improvement or causation.
