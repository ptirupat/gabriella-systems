Bowling Intelligence

Understand the complete bowling action

For bowling clips, Gabriella tracks the action through measurable stages and returns metrics and visualizations suited to that camera view — so coaches can review run-up speed, plus front-knee flexion at plant and arm angular speed where the take supports it.

Bowling phases

Every delivery broken into measurable stages — where the clip and view allow

Run-up

  • Peak approach speed (when pose is trusted)
  • Acceleration profile (where the approach is visible)
  • Same-view consistency (comparable takes only)

Gather

  • Body position into the stride
  • Stride characteristics (pose-dependent)
  • Alignment into plant (where visible)

Front-foot plant

  • Front-knee flexion at plant (0° ≈ fully extended)
  • Trunk position (where visible)
  • Stride length (pose-dependent)

Arm, ball & follow-through

  • Arm angular speed (when pose is ok)
  • Ball speed (quality-gated)
  • Follow-through alignment (where the bowler stays in frame)

What's live, conditional, and research-stage

Capability status for bowling analysis

Availability depends on camera view, calibration, visibility, and clip quality. The system reports "can't measure" when a metric cannot be reliably determined — it never substitutes a guess. Release height stays off this page until calibrated: no public metre, and no can't-measure slot.
